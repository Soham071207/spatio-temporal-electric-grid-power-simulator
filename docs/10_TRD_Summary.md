# Digital Twin & Chaos Engine Summary

A condensed version of the `spain_grid_gnn_digital_twin_project.md` representing our core objectives.

## Project Goal
Build a research-grade, data-calibrated **Digital Twin of the Spanish electricity system** to run Chaos Engineering simulations and train a GNN surrogate to predict grid resilience.

## Core Capabilities
- **Forecasting:** Demand and renewable generation.
- **Digital Twin:** A reduced-order graph where nodes are power plants and regions, and edges are transmission connections.
- **Chaos Engine:** Injecting failures (e.g., turning off a 1000MW plant, simulating a flood) to see how the system recalculates reserve margins and redispatches power.
- **GNN Surrogate:** Training an ST-GNN on thousands of synthetic failures so it learns to predict grid stability instantly, replacing expensive physics simulations.

## Important Constraints
- **Modelled Estimates:** We do not claim 100% real-time telemetry. If Plant A (1000MW) fails in simulation, we use its historical average (e.g., 820MW) to calculate the simulated lost generation.
- **Data Sources:** 
  - *REE/e-sios:* Demand, renewables, generation structure.
  - *ENTSO-E:* Unit-level generation telemetry and capacity.

## Updated Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Data):** Fetch demand, generation by technology, installed capacity, weather, and unit metadata. *(In Progress)*
- **Phase 2 (Forecasting):** Baselines and GNN temporal forecasting.
- **Phase 3 (Digital Twin):** Constructing the augmented Grid Graph.
- **Phase 4-7 (Chaos Engine):** Building failure simulators (Plant OFF, Weather disaster, Random/Adaptive scenarios) and redispatch logic.
- **Phase 8 (GNN Surrogate):** Train GNN on the synthetic contingency dataset generated by the Chaos Engine.
